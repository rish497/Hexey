# HEXEY 
Hello and welcome to my game _Hexey_. A mind-bending, level-based puzzler about overlapping computer windows to create traversable, top-down landscapes. Delve into the heart of an unusual operating system, one folder at a time!

**STORYLINE: **You get an email from an unknown person. They have leaked the images of aliens in Area51. But the CIA takes control of your computer and corrupts both the images and breaks them into your folders. The wizard then comes to your protection and saves one of the images. Your goal is to collect the parts of the images to reveal the secret image.

**DISCLAMER:** This game is very addicting!!!

Public Github Repo: https://github.com/rish497/Hexey
Itch Game Link: https://rish497.itch.io/hexey

**About Game 🎮**
Game was built in Godot
 Your out of place in the computer getting the image parts
The Main themes of the game are: Communication and Out of Place

**Theme Usage ⭐**
Communication: You are communicating through folders in the game and collecting the broken parts of the image. 
Out Of place: Rather than a platformer where you are the character in game. You are the person on the computer which got hacked by the CIA, and you and controlling the Character to solve the mystery image.
When Physic's fails: The platforms on which the character walks can be moved.

**Functionality 👾**
Use W,S,A,D or Arrow keys to move
Join the hexagon tiles to make your map
Collect the image parts in the tiles
You can drag the click the tiles. Clicking the tiles brings them a layer up.
GOAL: To collect 500bits
The Buttons on the TaskBar: Menu, Folders (to access the game play), Sound control, How to Play, Wizard Popup.
When playing the level 2 new buttons will be on the TaskBar: Play, Reset
Enjoy the Game
