A level-based puzzle game where you overlap and drag computer windows to create playable, top-down landscapes inside a strange operating system.

## Story

You receive leaked alien images from Area 51.  
The CIA hacks your computer and corrupts the files, scattering them across your folders.  
A wizard saves one fragment.  
Your goal is to collect all image parts and reveal the secret image.

## Gameplay

- Move with **WASD** or **Arrow Keys**
- Drag and layer hex tiles to build paths
- Collect image fragments
- Reach **500 Bits** to win
